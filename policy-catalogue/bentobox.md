## Policy substance (mapped to schema fields)

| Schema field | This project's position |
| --- | --- |
| stance (code) | unrestricted — contributors may use AI tools without restriction or limitation |
| stance (docs / translations / media) | Documentation and media: unstated. Translations: restricted to PRs from native speakers; AI may assist a native speaker, but AI-generated translations alone are not accepted. |
| disclosure required? mechanism? | No. Code contributors are told they may disclose AI use; the policy says maintainers assume it anyway. |
| attestations required | No formal attestation. In substance, contributors must understand the code, answer review questions about it, and fix it if asked until acceptance (`understands-change`). |
| enforcement on non-disclosure | None stated; disclosure is optional. Ordinary review applies regardless of AI use: unsatisfactory PRs may be rejected or changes requested. |
| first-time contributors | No separate gate documented. The contributor guide welcomes PRs and asks contributors to keep them focused and pass available tests. |

## Verbatim excerpt

> “Anyone contributing to BentoBox can use AI tools. There is no restriction or limitation.”

## Notes

The policy applies across BentoBoxWorld repositories. It also permits AI-generated bug reports and feature requests, with a note that this may change if they become onerous; contributions remain subject to EPL-2.0 and the Code of Conduct regardless of AI use.
